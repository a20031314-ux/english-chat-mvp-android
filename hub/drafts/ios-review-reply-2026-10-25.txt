2. Purpose and audience
Languagebank is a language-learning app for adults and teens aged 14 and older who want to actually speak and write a new language. Learners chat and talk out loud with an AI tutor in the language they are learning. When they are stuck they can write in their own language and see how to say it, and each sentence they write is corrected. They can also learn from short curated videos with sentence-by-sentence subtitles. There are no levels: the app shows only what was wrong or missing, and a study map built from what the learner has written. 14 learning languages and 14 app languages are supported.

3. Setup and access
No login is required. All main features (Chat, Call, Video, My) work right after launch, once the user accepts the AI data consent screen. Creating an account is optional and only keeps progress across devices (My > "Sign in to keep your progress" > email). No demo account or sample files are needed.

4. External services
- Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI: AI tutor replies, corrections, explanations and translations; OpenAI also provides speech-to-text and the tutor's voice during calls. The user agrees to this on the first-launch consent screen, and it is described in the Privacy Policy.
- AssemblyAI / ElevenLabs: backup speech-to-text providers used only if the primary transcription service is unavailable.
- RevenueCat with Apple In-App Purchase: subscriptions and point purchases. All payments go through Apple.
- Resend: sends the verification email for optional account sign-up.
- Vercel (server hosting) and Upstash (database): store account data and learned expressions.
- YouTube: the monthly videos play in the official YouTube embedded player.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions. Only the interface language changes, following the user's choice. Content and features do not differ by region.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
The app is not in a regulated industry. The videos are public YouTube videos played through YouTube's official embedded player under the YouTube Terms of Service. The app does not download, re-host or modify them.

7. In-App Purchases and how to reach them
Tap "Plan" at the top right of any screen to open Plan & billing.
- Auto-renewable monthly subscriptions (one group):
  Lite (lite_monthly) - daily free call minutes and 30 chats a day
  Plus (plus_monthly) - more daily call minutes and 50 chats a day
  Premium (premium_monthly) - the most call minutes, 100 chats a day, the full monthly video library and extra daily packs
- Consumable points (points_30, points_60, points_200, points_500) - used for extra call time after the daily minutes run out. They are on the same screen under "Top up points".
"Restore purchases" is on the same screen.
