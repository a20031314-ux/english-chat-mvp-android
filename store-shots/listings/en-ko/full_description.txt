Start speaking Korean with languagebank. Call and chat with an AI tutor in Korean: it shows you the corrected line right away, and a daily quiz locks in what you learn. New to Hangul? Start with letters and sounds.

Learn with an AI tutor
• Call: talk freely with your tutor. A coach shows corrections as captions under your face; say it again and it checks right away. Homework comes when something was corrected.
• Chat: corrected sentences show up right away. Stuck? Write in your own language and the tutor carries on in the one you are learning.

Many kinds of quizzes (Quizbank)
• Choose, meaning, find the error and write the sentence, all in one flow.
• It gets harder as you get answers right, and what you missed or got stuck on in conversation comes back.
• Make it easier or harder yourself.

Every word to learn, in one place (Languagebank)
• Browse words, expressions and grammar by type and level, and learn each set with a quiz or a conversation.
• Flip cards to memorize meanings, words and examples.

Watch what you learn add up (Logbank)
• A learning curve shows what you studied in quizzes and what you used in conversation, day by day.
• Look back at what you did each day, hour by hour.

Learn with other people
• Connect with learners of the same language through profiles, the board and messages, and talk by voice in a Room.

Getting started
• A short check on first launch sets your starting level. There are no level scores.

Pricing
• Quizbank, Languagebank and lessons are free (with ads). Try 10 minutes of calls and 10 chat messages; Lite, Plus and Premium remove ads and add daily call time and chat messages.
