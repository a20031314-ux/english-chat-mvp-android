Word traits (gender, aspect, classifiers, verb classes, irregular forms) — languagebank, src/lib/curriculum/word-traits/
Produced offline from the open sources below, then checked and completed by Claude (Anthropic). Not yet reviewed by native speakers.

ru  gender, aspect — pymorphy3 with OpenCorpora dictionaries (https://opencorpora.org), CC BY-SA 3.0
zh  classifiers — CC-CEDICT (https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict) via pycccedict, CC BY-SA 4.0; frequent nouns missing there filled by Claude
ja  verb and adjective classes — fugashi with UniDic (unidic-lite, NINJAL, BSD/LGPL/GPL); keigo pairs by Claude
ko  irregular conjugation classes and honorific pairs — rules and exception lists by Claude
en  irregular verb forms and plurals — LemmInflect (https://github.com/bjascob/LemmInflect), MIT; uncountable nouns by Claude
