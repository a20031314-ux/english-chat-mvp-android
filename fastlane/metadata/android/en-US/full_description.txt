languagebank is one app for learning a language: sentence patterns, practice, and speaking out loud. Learn any of 14 languages, with the app in any of 14 languages.

1. All your language learning in one place. Stagebank holds Today (5, 10, or 20 minutes) and sentence structures. Quizbank is where you practice. Conversation is where you use it. Mistakes come back as quizzes.
2. Get the sentence patterns down. Sentence structures shows a short grammar page, then you type only the key piece.
3. Practice until it sticks. In Quizbank you type the blank. After a choice, each option shows its meaning and why the others are wrong.
4. Talk out loud with an AI tutor. The call looks like a phone call. Tap the caption for a translation. Tutor lesson speaks a big mistake so you can say it again. "I didn't catch that" asks to hear it once more.
5. After the call, just what to fix. What you meant comes back as a correct sentence.
6. Learn with others studying the same language: group calls, group chats, and Room.
7. Mistakes come back as quizzes. A line fixed on a call becomes that pattern's lesson, then review.
8. Skip what you know. Logbank opens on Language skills. Tap a corner, and what you need comes first.

Also
• Stuck in chat? Write in your own language. The tutor continues in the one you're learning, and "Show how to say it" shows the line you wanted.
• Sentence analysis: pick the part you don't get and ask what it means.
• Video: real videos split into sentences to replay and ask about.
• You can study more than one language. The tutor has a photo and a female or male voice.

No level names
• Each sentence gets its mistakes pointed out. Language skills shows a percent on each corner.

Pricing
• Stage scripts are free, with ads. The Scripts plan adds short calls and chat. Lite, Plus, and Premium remove ads and add more chat and daily call time.
