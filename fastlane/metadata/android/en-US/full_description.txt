languagebank helps you actually speak and write in the language you're learning — with an AI tutor, real videos and other learners. Learn any of 14 languages, with the app in any of 14 languages.

TALK WITH AN AI TUTOR
• Chat in the language you're learning. Stuck? Write in your own language: the tutor keeps going in the language you're learning, and "Show how to say it" puts the expression right under that line.
• Corrections appear right under your sentence, so you see exactly what to fix.
• Call the AI tutor and just talk out loud. Today's pack gives you a short call with a goal, and afterwards you can look back at what you meant to say.

ASK ABOUT ANY SENTENCE
• Open sentence analysis on a line from chat, calls, videos or your notes. Pick the part you don't get and ask what it means. Answers come in your own language.

LEARN FROM REAL VIDEOS
• Videos are split into sentences with subtitles in the language you're learning. Tap a sentence to replay it, and ask about any line.

SEE WHAT YOU'RE MISSING
• No levels. Each sentence shows only what was wrong or missing, and what you've written shows where you're weak and what to do next.
• Set a goal and get a study map with missions. Hints open one at a time, so you try first.
• In My: Try this, Growing, My phrases and Notes (with Vocab) gather what you've used and learned.

LEARN WITH PEOPLE
• Room: voice rooms with live captions. Tap a line to translate it, or pick just the words you want to ask about. A 💡 hint shows you what to say in the language you're learning.
• Chats: group chats and 1:1 messages, voice calls and 1:1 video calls, and team spaces with study mates.
• People: find people learning your language, follow them and post on the board.
• Invite a friend with one link.

PLANS
Free to start, with daily limits. The free plan shows ads. Subscriptions add more chats, more AI help and daily call time, without ads.
